# Morning intel brief

Produce a dated brief of the most important AI and technology developments since the last brief.

Rules:
- Use only sources reachable from this machine (web search or configured feed tools). If no research tool is available, say so and produce the brief skeleton with a "no sources checked" note instead of inventing headlines.
- Every claim carries its source link. Never fabricate a headline, number, or quote.
- Save the brief under `output/` as `YYYY-MM-DD-morning-intel.md` with frontmatter `date`, `generated`, and `sources_checked`.
- After saving, update `metrics/audience.toml` only with real, user-entered values. Do not invent metric values.
- This skill is read-only: it drafts a brief. It never sends, publishes, or spends.
